## Recommended Demo Order

1. Admin opens the July dashboard and shows users, races, deposits, betting,
   incidents and unpublished results.
2. Horse Owner registers one horse in `Independence Cup Open Entry Demo` and
   completes VNPay.
3. Horse Owner starts the primary-jockey invitation flow for the new entry.
4. Jockey accepts the appointment, terms and primary contract.
5. Spectator opens `Capital Classic Heat 2`, reviews odds and places a win bet.
6. Referee opens `King Edward VII Stakes Demo` and completes all pre-checks.
7. Referee starts the race while Spectator watches the 2D animation.
8. Referee records a during-race violation and confirms or adjusts the suggested
   penalty.
9. Referee completes the race, records post-checks and submits the report.
10. Referee finalizes draft results and applies confirmed penalties.
11. Admin reviews and publishes the official result.
12. Horse Owner, Jockey and Spectator review final positions, penalties, prize
    information and settled betting data.
